Hospital Management System — Role-based Prototype

Active Role: Patient
Patient can view own records & prescriptions only.
Demo data & UI to demonstrate abstraction, information hiding, cohesion & modularity

Quick Info

This sidebar shows which UI elements are visible to each role (information hiding) and explains design choices.

  • Doctor: Full patient access, edit health records, view billing & prescriptions.
  • Nurse: Update daily reports and vitals; cannot change diagnoses or billing.
  • Patient: View-only access to their personal record & prescriptions.
  • Administrator: Manage users, roles, and system settings.

Cohesion & Modularity

Subsystems are separated: Records, Appointments, Billing, Pharmacy. Each subsystem has its own UI and responsibilities (high cohesion).

Notes: This is a front-end prototype simulating role-based information hiding. No real backend or authentication is present.
