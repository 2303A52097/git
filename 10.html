Comparative Report — Strengths & Weaknesses (Concise)

  • Monolith — Strengths: simple deploy; easier local dev; less cross-service latency. Weaknesses: hard to scale components independently; large codebase risk; slower CI/CD on each change.
  • Microservices — Strengths: independent scaling & deploy; technology heterogeneity; smaller service codebases. Weaknesses: higher operational complexity; network latency; need for service discovery, monitoring, distributed tracing.

When to choose

Start with Monolith for small teams / MVP. Move to Microservices when team scale, independent scaling needs, or organizational boundaries require it.
